# Oh My CPA v0.4.14

Oh My CPA v0.4.14 adds subscription billing-cycle usage totals and full-cycle pace projections, admits observed execution models into pricing, fixes prefixed and hyphenated OAuth model-alias discovery, hardens verification and test isolation, and refreshes documentation screenshots.

## Subscription Billing-Cycle Usage Totals

- **Verified subscription period tracking & projection.** Persists optional `starts_at_ms` on `QuotaPlan` (`quota_snapshots.plan_json`) from upstream Codex (`active_start` / JWT `chatgpt_subscription_active_start`) and Devin (`cycle_start`) responses without a schema migration, and resolves active `[starts_at_ms, expires_at_ms)` and previous billing cycles in `internal/quota/subscription_cycle.go` while preserving month-end billing anchors and guarding linear pace projections with minimum elapsed-time and traffic checks.
- **Billing-cycle API, demo parity & console dialog.** Adds `GET /api/v1/management/quota/{authIndex}/subscription-cycle` (`internal/api/management_quota_subscription_cycle.go`, `docs/openapi/swagger.json`, `internal/demo`, `deploy/cloudflare`) backed by permanent `usage_facts_*` snapshots (`QueryUsageFactsSnapshot`) so totals survive `usage_events` detail pruning while keeping adjacent 15-minute buckets disjoint and returning `404` when no verified active subscription period exists. Adds a lazy-loaded **Subscription cycle** tab in `QuotaCapacityDialog` and a direct toolbar trigger in `CredentialQuotaBody` with height-stabilized 3-card layout across all four locales (`zh`, `en`, `zh-Hant`, `ms`).

## Observed Model Pricing & Prefixed OAuth Aliases

- **Observed execution models in pricing.** Admits models and `(channel, model)` pairs observed in traffic (`usage_facts_daily`, `usage_facts_15m`, and unfolded `usage_events`) into pricing validation (`internal/pricing`, `internal/repository`, `internal/api`, `internal/operations`) while still rejecting unseen models and avoiding guesswork across reasoning-effort suffixes. Surfaces unpriced observed models in `UsedUnpricedModels`, preserves active observed automatic prices across catalog refreshes within `observedActivityWindow`, adds `GetPrice` lookup fallback for older retained requests, fingerprints all stored prices in write revision checks, and prevents opening the price editor for `"unknown"` placeholders in `RequestRow.tsx`, `CostBreakdownView.tsx`, and `ModelUsagePanels.tsx`.
- **Prefixed and hyphenated OAuth model-alias discovery.** Updates `internal/cpa/management` and `internal/operations/model_square.go` to decode both `model_aliases` and legacy `model-aliases` plus `prefix` (zeroing downloaded auth-file bytes), strip credential prefixes before `ResolveOAuthModelAlias`, and preserve resolved `provider.Routes` in `ProjectModelSquare`.

## Verification, CI & Documentation

- **Test isolation & faster verification gates.** Isolates in-memory SQLite test databases per `Open()`, re-enables `freshBootstrapSQL` with full schema and seed parity checks, pins OpenUI capability locale coverage via `internal/agent/openui/registered_capabilities.json`, drains pending route-loader imports in `web/tests/setup.ts`, removes the `tests/logic` exclusion from `web/tsconfig.json`, and runs `gofmt`, TypeScript, Vitest `logic`, and `go test -short` concurrently through `scripts/run-gates.mjs`.
- **Documentation & screenshots.** Clarifies observed pricing membership in `docs/pricing.md` and `docs/zh/pricing.md`, and updates English and Chinese console screenshots under `docs/images/readme/`.

## Upgrade notes

- Back up `.env` and your SQLite database or volume before upgrading; refer to `docs/ops/sqlite-operations.md`.
- No database migrations, new required environment variables, or Compose changes are introduced in v0.4.14.
- Container images are available on Docker Hub as `wiziscool/oh-my-cpa:v0.4.14` and `wiziscool/oh-my-cpa:0.4.14`.
