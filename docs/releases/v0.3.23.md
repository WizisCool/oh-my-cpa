# Oh My CPA v0.3.23

This release makes the console's side panels and motion feel like one product: every
drawer resizes from its edge and remembers its width, routing tiers fold and dashboard
edits glide under a single motion system, and the OAuth and AI-provider pages tighten
into badges and phone cards. Pricing learns image models, plugins show the quota
amounts their publishers write, and the repository's own test and CI setup is rebuilt
for speed. A new plugin, `omc-zcode-oauth`, joins the registry.

## Resizable drawers and shared motion

- Side panels resize from their leading edge. The chosen width is stored per panel in
  the `omc_drawer_widths` preference; the console clamps it to the window when it
  draws the panel, so a narrower window does not overwrite a wide monitor's choice.
  Keyboard resizes are announced from a live region.
- Motion arrives through one module (`web/src/motion`): every animated surface imports
  it, runs under the same reduced-motion policy, and settles with the same few
  critically damped springs — things leave a little quicker than they arrive. The
  animation features load with the surface's own lazy route chunk, so pages that do
  not animate never carry the library.
- Routing tiers fold in and out with that motion, and dashboard layout editing glides
  and fades: moving a widget animates it into its slot, removing one fades it out and
  restores it if the drop is cancelled.
- Holding a credential row at the window's bottom edge no longer slides the whole
  console out of the window: the content area now contains absolutely positioned
  descendants, so a drag auto-scrolls the workspace and never the page.

## OAuth, providers and records

- Plugin quota buckets may carry a publisher-written `description` and payload a
  `summary` of metrics; both are kept and shown in the quota card, the dashboard
  meter tooltip and the diagnostics. The text is bounded (descriptions to 160 runes,
  labels to 48, at most 8 metrics, currency only as an ISO code) because it belongs
  to the plugin, not to OMC. Previously a credit-based plugin showed only a bar.
- The OAuth workspace groups auth files by CPA's `oauth_provider` entry when a plugin
  declares two or more portals sharing one type, and falls back to the current
  grouping until CPA reports the field.
- The OAuth list fits its rows in two lines: "Reauthenticate" is the status link,
  priority and weight appear as an inline badge beside the name, and the SIWC state
  moved into the status column. The off/disabled glyph is a no-entry sign instead of
  a media "stop" square.
- The AI-providers table shows models, keys and headers as compact badges with
  structured tooltips, sized to fit 1440px. On phones the list becomes framed cards —
  name with priority, route with host, health with 24h traffic, a switch and a ⋯
  menu — and the summary tiles become a segmented control.
- The request record drawer is redesigned as an overview on the page ground, with
  fact lists separated by rhythm instead of rules (dividers had disappeared in dark
  mode). The credential drawer adds an overview tab whose hero shows the saved
  priority and weight, with Save in the footer.

## Plugin store

- The store draws every registry entry's artwork — installed or not — under one
  bounded rule: an inline image or an absolute HTTP(S) URL without credentials.
  Card marks load lazily without a referrer, and the demo still withholds external
  artwork.
- The maintenance card keeps the outcome the page observed for the job it belongs to,
  and keeps the verdict when the panel is dismissed. A terminal record the page
  cannot attribute ends the poll, and a poll still owed by a restarted process is
  cancelled instead of being attributed to this process's job.
- `omc-zcode-oauth` joins the OMC plugin registry at v0.2.2: a native ZCode provider
  for CPA with dual-portal Z.ai / BigModel OAuth login, model discovery, Anthropic
  Messages execution, Coding Plan and gift routing, and normalized quota.

## Pricing

- The OpenRouter catalog is fetched with `output_modalities=all`, so pure
  image-generation models are available for pricing and exact matching. A model whose
  only output modality is `image` is priced by its `image_output` token rate —
  explicit zero included — and tier overrides use the same rate key; mixed text/image
  models keep completion pricing, and legacy entries without modality metadata are
  unchanged. Other newly discovered non-text output types stay unpriced until their
  billing policy is supported.
- `gpt-image-2`, `gpt-image-2.5-*` and `gpt-image-2.6-*` now resolve to their own
  OpenRouter entries. Operator-owned custom prices and links remain authoritative,
  newly available exact matches arrive through the existing review flow, and stored
  request costs and their explanations are untouched.

## Configuration panels and copy

- Form fields render label, then control, then helper, so inputs share one horizontal
  baseline across columns regardless of the description's length. Switches inside
  form grids match the height of text inputs, number inputs and selects, and host/port
  combos align their labels and inputs on the same baselines. Empty description
  wrappers are omitted and `aria-describedby` binds only when a description exists.
- Sixty-nine self-explanatory subtitles were removed across ports, TLS paths, standard
  toggles and endpoints, and retained descriptions no longer enumerate fallback
  conditions or lecture about restarts — in all four locales, and across drawer and
  page catalogs beyond configuration.
- Loading skeletons keep their spacing below the heading on phones: placeholder page
  heads no longer lose their padding to the mobile `display: contents` rule.

## Development and CI

- The test pyramid is consolidated: unit and logic tests live in `web/tests/logic/`
  with shared fixtures, 30 micro-test files and 12 single-issue JSDOM component tests
  were deleted, and component suites shrank from over 8,000 lines to about 2,200.
  Frontend tests run in about 20 seconds instead of 109.
- Mocked dev-server Playwright probes are gone; browser acceptance runs against the
  real compiled Go binary, embedded SPA, SQLite and a fake CPA, and CI limits PR runs
  to the built-artifact smoke. CI splits into four on-demand parallel lanes (`go`,
  `frontend`, `static`, `browser`).
- All technical documentation and code comments are now English-only (Chinese remains
  in `README.zh-CN.md` and the UI translation catalogs), and the agent guidelines in
  `AGENTS.md` codify the engineering and autonomy rules this release follows.

## Upgrade notes

- Back up `.env` and the data directory or volume before upgrading; follow
  `docs/ops/sqlite-operations.md`.
- No schema migration: the database stays at version 057.
- No environment variable or Compose change is required. Compose continues to default
  to `latest`. After publication, pin this release with `wiziscool/oh-my-cpa:v0.3.23`
  or `wiziscool/oh-my-cpa:0.3.23`.
- The new `omc_drawer_widths` preference records panel widths you drag; existing
  preferences keep their saved values.
- Automatic pricing may newly resolve pure image models. Existing custom prices and
  links stay authoritative, and historical costs do not change.
- Multi-portal OAuth grouping activates once CPA reports `oauth_provider` on the
  auth-file list; until then the console groups as before.
