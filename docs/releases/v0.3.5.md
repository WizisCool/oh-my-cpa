# Oh My CPA v0.3.5

This release adds explicit service-tier pricing, so requests answered on a
provider's `priority` or `fast` lane can use that lane's rates rather than the
standard price. It also makes manual request-refresh decode counts independent
of background decoder timing.

## Service-tier pricing

- Price tiers can name upstream service tiers through `service_tiers`. Names are
  trimmed and compared case-insensitively; list `priority` and `fast` together only
  when the provider charges them at the same rate.
- Pricing uses the service tier reported in the response, falling back to the
  requested tier when no concrete response tier is available. Empty and `auto`
  values do not select a service-tier rule. Requests without a matching lane use
  the existing standard pricing rules.
- Exactly one tier sets the rates: service-tier rules take precedence, then the
  highest applicable input-token threshold, then a matching time window, then
  stored order. A lane can carry its own long-context and time-of-day steps;
  conditions must all match and rates never stack. Omitted rates inherit the
  base price, explicit zero rates remain free, and model/channel multipliers
  still apply once at the end.
- A price supports up to 16 tiers, with up to eight service-tier names per tier.

## Console and agent capabilities

- The price editor adds service-tier lanes with fixed rates or multiples of the
  standard rates, lane-specific steps and a lane-aware quote preview.
- Price badges and ladders identify the service tier. Request cost breakdowns
  show the billed tier and whether it came from the response or the request.
- Agent/MCP `pricing_quote` accepts `service_tier`;
  `requests_cost_breakdown` includes `service_tier` and `service_tier_source`.
- The demonstration price book includes a priority lane for `minimax-m3`.

## Request refresh counts

- Manual refresh counts decoded records within its own capture range, including
  records the background decoder finished before the refresh barrier began.
- The refresh still waits for captured records to become queryable before
  reporting success. Older backlog and later captures do not inflate its decoded
  count.

## Upgrade notes

- Back up `.env` and the data directory or volume before upgrading; follow
  `docs/ops/sqlite-operations.md`. This release adds no SQL migration; the schema
  remains at `043_channel_model_prices.sql`.
- Existing prices without service-tier conditions retain their stored form and
  pricing behavior. Recorded costs stay locked to their ingestion-time price
  versions; changing lane prices does not recalculate historical costs.
- Configure lane prices explicitly from the provider's published rate card.
  Service-tier pricing does not automatically assign a surcharge to priority
  requests or infer that different provider tier names share a rate.
- No new environment variables are introduced. OMC's CPA v8.0.0 Management API
  baseline is unchanged; response-tier attribution depends on what CPA reports.
- Compose continues to default to `latest`. After publication, pin this release
  with `wiziscool/oh-my-cpa:v0.3.5` or `wiziscool/oh-my-cpa:0.3.5`.
