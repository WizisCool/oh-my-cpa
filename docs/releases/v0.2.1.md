# Oh My CPA v0.2.1

## Dashboard

- The activity row pairs the token grid with a **credential-quota** panel: every enabled
  credential by urgency, the ones that cannot serve first. A credential that cannot serve
  states why in place of its windows — sign-in required, cooling down, unavailable, or no
  reading at all — and when it recovers; an exhausted one keeps its windows, all at zero, with
  the countdown to when it serves again. One that can serve draws its plan's two shortest quota
  windows as remaining-share bars, with the countdown to the tightest window refilling. Disabled
  credentials are counted in the header. The list scrolls inside the panel, so a large fleet is
  readable without the panel outgrowing the row it shares, and its rows are not links: a touch
  meant to move the list cannot leave the page. Its one control re-reads those quotas from the
  providers, and its footer opens `/oauth-management`.
- The year of weeks now shows as many of the newest weeks as fit at a comfortable cell size and
  scrolls to the older ones behind a pinned weekday gutter, with a key naming the ramp's
  direction. A square stays readable and tappable in the half-width column at every width,
  including a phone, where the field still opens on today.
- The dashboard's runtime information panel is gone. The counts a reader acts on are in the
  quota panel and the page header, and the system page owns the instance and version facts.

## Public demonstration

- The demonstration replays one recorded conversation per workspace. The Agent example streams a
  recorded run — the transcript, the capability timeline and the generated interface are the
  production ones — and the Playground example streams a recorded answer through its own wire
  contract. Neither sends an inference request or a server write, and only the offered example
  question is accepted.
- The dashboard reads like a real deployment: the dataset carries a full synthetic year of
  traffic with a steady trend, idle days and quieter weekends.
- Exported conversations keep their structure. A saved answer keeps the order of its reasoning,
  capability calls and figures, and the PNG draws each generated interface in the same opaque
  sandbox the console's own "save as image" uses, instead of the fallback note it used to show.
- The demonstration announces itself with an arrival toast rather than holding a banner row.

## Console fixes

- The phone dashboard's token grid opens on the newest date rather than the left edge.
- The navigation rail is revealed by keyboard focus as well as by pointer.
- The provider icon catalog is synced to the installed Lucide release.

## Upgrade notes

- Back up the persistent SQLite database and preserve its master key before upgrading; follow
  `docs/ops/sqlite-operations.md`. This release adds no database migration beyond v0.2.0 and does
  not change its retention defaults.
- The dashboard's credential-quota panel reads `GET /management/auth-files` and
  `GET /management/quota`. A management key that may not read both leaves the panel in its
  unknown state with a retry; nothing else on the dashboard depends on those reads.
- Compose continues to default to `latest`. To pin this release after publication, use
  `wiziscool/oh-my-cpa:v0.2.1` or `wiziscool/oh-my-cpa:0.2.1`.
